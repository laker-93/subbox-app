Listen on your phone

Your Sub-box library is also a Subsonic music server, so any Subsonic app on your iPhone or Android phone can play it: your tracks, albums and playlists, streamed or downloaded for offline listening.

Upload some music first. The phone app plays what’s already in your Sub-box library. If it’s empty, follow one of the computer guides first.

Part 2 of 3

Find your server address

Your address is your Sub-box username inside https://navidromeusername.sub-box.net.

https://navidromeyourusername.sub-box.net
Part 3 of 3

Connect the app

  1. Open the app and add a server. It asks for one the first time it opens. If it asks what kind, choose Navidrome if it’s listed, otherwise Subsonic.

  2. Enter your server address, exactly as above: https://navidromeyourusername.sub-box.net. No port number, nothing after .net.

  3. Enter your username and password. They’re the ones you sign in to Sub-box with: your Sub-box username and your Sub-box password.

  4. Save, or tap Log in. The app loads your library. A big library can take a minute the first time.

Where to find it in each app

Amperfy: the login screen opens by itself. Fill in Server URL, Username and Password, then tap Login.

substreamer: on first launch, add a server: give it any name (“Sub-box”), then the address, username and password.

play:Sub: open Settings, then the server settings, and enter the address, username and password.

Symfonium: on first launch, add a media provider, choose Subsonic (or Navidrome), then the address, username and password.

Good to know

What you’ll see on your phone

  • Playlists inside folders show their folder in the name, like Gigs / Warm Up.
  • Cue points, loops and beat grids stay in Sub-box and your DJ software. Phone apps just play the tracks.
  • Music you upload later shows up once Sub-box has finished importing it. If it doesn’t appear, refresh the library in the app’s settings.
  • Stars and ratings you give in the app are saved to your Sub-box library.
If something goes wrong

Common problems

It can’t reach the server

Check the address starts with https://, has your username straight after navidrome with no space or dot, and ends at .sub-box.net. Some apps add a port number such as :4533 by themselves: remove it.

It says the username or password is wrong

Use the same username and password as the Sub-box app. If you can sign in there but not on your phone, ask us on Discord.

It connects but the library is empty

Sign in to the Sub-box app and check your tracks are under Library. If they are, refresh the library in the phone app’s settings.
