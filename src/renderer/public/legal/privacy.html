1. Who is responsible for your data

For anything in this notice, contact luke.a.purnell@gmail.com.

2. What we collect, why, and on what legal basis

Account data

Your username, email address and password, plus internal identifiers for your account and the personal server containers we run for you.

Why: to create and operate your account. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.

Your music library

The audio files you upload and their embedded metadata (title, artist, album, and an identifier tag Subbox writes into each file), your playlists, cue points and other DJ-workflow data, and records of the original filenames and metadata of files you imported.

Why: this is the service — storing your library and moving it between your DJ software and your player. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.

Usage of your own library

Play counts, timestamps of recent plays, favourites and star ratings, held by the personal music server we run for you.

Why: to give you features that depend on them, such as recently played and most played. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.

Wishlist data

Tracks you add to your wishlist, including any links you paste (for example YouTube, Bandcamp or SoundCloud URLs) and the metadata we match them against.

Why: to operate the wishlist feature. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.

Job and diagnostic records

Records of imports, exports and other long-running operations on your library, and server logs from the web server and application (which include IP addresses, times and what was requested).

Why: to show you progress, to diagnose faults, and to keep the Service secure. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you, and our legitimate interest in a secure and working service.

Service metrics

Operational metrics from the personal music server we run for you — such as request counts, library sizes and error rates — labelled with your account so that we can tell whose server is unhealthy. These are sent to our monitoring provider.

Why: to keep the Service running and to spot faults. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a reliable service.

Beta invitation requests

If you ask for a beta invitation we store the email address you give us, which DJ software you say you use, and when you asked.

Why: to run the invitation list. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in managing access to a limited beta, and steps taken at your request before entering a contract. Your IP address is held briefly in memory to rate-limit the form and is not written to our database.

Product analytics

None. Subbox does not use product analytics, and does not load any third-party tracking or advertising script. We do not build a profile of how you use the app, and nothing about your usage is sent to anyone else.

3. Cookies and local storage

When you log in we set a session cookie so that the Service knows it is you. This cookie is strictly necessary for the Service to work and cannot be turned off.

The application also stores your settings and preferences locally in your browser or desktop app. That data stays on your device.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We use the following providers, who process data on our behalf:

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, or to establish or defend legal claims.

5. Sending data outside the UK

The Service is hosted in the United Kingdom. Your account, the music you upload and the service metrics we collect are all held in UK datacentres, so using Subbox does not by itself send your data overseas.

Two things do leave the UK:

  • Traffic routing. Cloudflare, Inc. sits in front of the Service and routes your requests to it, so your IP address and the details of your request pass through its network, which spans the United States and other countries. For this we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, which forms part of Cloudflare's data processing terms.
  • Email. If you write to us, your message is received and stored in a mailbox operated by Google LLC in the United States, and our reply is sent from there.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account data and your library for as long as your account is open. When your account is closed we delete your library and your account record. Any backup copies still holding your data are deleted as those backups are rotated out.

Server logs are kept for 14 days. Service metrics are kept for 14 days. Beta invitation requests are kept until the beta closes or you ask us to remove yours.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law — and, if you are in the EU, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation — you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • receive the data you gave us in a portable format, or have it sent to another provider where technically feasible;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To exercise any of these, email luke.a.purnell@gmail.com. We will respond within one month. There is no charge unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

You can download your own library from within the app at any time. Deletion of your account and library is currently handled by us on request rather than by a button in the app.

8. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first at luke.a.purnell@gmail.com so we can try to put it right.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or, if you are in the EU, to the data protection authority in the country where you live.

9. Security

Your library is stored in a container that is separate from other users' libraries, and traffic to the Service is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure; please keep your own backups of music you care about, and use a password you do not use anywhere else.

10. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact luke.a.purnell@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this notice

If we change this notice materially we will tell you by email or in the app before the change takes effect. The version and date at the top always show the current version.
