Getting started

Get your music onto Sub-box

Two ways in: drag a few tracks into the app to try it, or go straight to your Rekordbox or Serato library and bring its playlists and cue points with it. Each guide takes you from downloading the app to hearing your music in Sub-box, in about ten minutes plus upload time.
