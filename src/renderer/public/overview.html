Sub-box
What is Sub-box

Your DJ library, wherever you are

A cloud copy of your music, with the playlists, cue points and beat grids you made in Rekordbox or Serato. Play and prep it anywhere, then take the work back to your DJ software.

Get started

Prepare tracks

Prep away from your DJ laptop

Cues and loops on a full waveformSet hot cues and loops in the prep view.
  • The whole track at the top, a zoomed waveform with its beat grid below
  • Double-click to set a hot cue, drag to move it
  • Shift-drag to make a loop
  • Name and colour each cue and loop
  • Snap to the beat, the bar, or not at all
Every edit is a versionUndo, redo or go back to any earlier version.
  • Every change to a track’s cues and loops is saved
  • Undo and redo as you work
  • Revert a track to any earlier version, including how it came in from Rekordbox or Serato
Take the work back into Rekordbox or SeratoYour prep goes back with the playlist.
  • Download a playlist as a Rekordbox XML or as Serato crates
  • Cues, loops and beat grids set in Sub-box go with it

Prepping tracks is part of DJ mode. Sub-box offers to turn it on when an import brings cues with it, or you can switch it on in Settings.

Keep it safe

Nothing is lost by mistake

Undo a deleteDeleted playlists, folders and tracks can come back for 7 days.
  • Delete a playlist, a folder or a track and undo it for 7 days
  • Until then it waits in Recently deleted
  • If importing your library again replaces a playlist, you can put the old one back
Edit on more than one computerConflicting changes are never quietly overwritten.
  • Change the same track on two devices and Sub-box asks which change to keep
A copy that isn’t on your laptopA lost laptop doesn’t take your library with it.
  • Your music, playlists and prep live in Sub-box as well as on your DJ laptop
Your library

One library, every format

Rekordbox and Serato in one placeBring a library in from one, take it out as the other.
  • Serato in, Rekordbox out, or the other way round
  • Playlists, crates and folders come across
  • So do cue points, loops and beat grids
Listen anywhereDesktop app, web browser or your phone.
  • Play your own playlists in the desktop app or a web browser
  • On your phone, use any Subsonic-compatible music app
  • Rediscover tracks on the way to a gig
Never lose a track you heardKeep a wishlist from links or a Google Sheet.
  • Paste a YouTube, SoundCloud or Bandcamp link to a track, playlist or album
  • Sub-box adds it to your wishlist with the artist and title filled in
  • Away from the app, jot tracks into a Google Sheet on your phone, even offline
  • They join the same wishlist
Your DJ data in the track listSort by BPM, key and cues, as in your DJ software.
  • BPM, key (Camelot, musical or both) and cue counts as columns
  • Sort your library the way you would in Rekordbox or Serato
Try it

Start with a few tracks

  • No Rekordbox or Serato needed: drag a few tracks into the app
  • Listen to them somewhere else
  • When you’re ready, bring in a playlist from your DJ software and prep one track’s cues
